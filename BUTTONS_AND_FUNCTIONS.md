# AutoSub AI Studio: Buttons & Functions Guide

Welcome to **AutoSub AI Studio**, your all-in-one suite for AI-powered subtitles, lyrics, and DJ tools. This guide explains how to use the application.

---

## 🏠 Dashboard (Home)

The Dashboard is your starting point. You can quickly launch any of the three main modules.

### 1. Script Input
- **What it is:** A text area to paste your video script or song lyrics.
- **How it works:** When you upload a file, the AI uses this script to improve transcription accuracy and alignment.
- **Action:** Paste your text before uploading your media.

### 2. Upload Zone
- **What it is:** A drag-and-drop area for your media files.
- **Supported Formats:** MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV (up to 50MB).
- **Action:** Drag a file or click to select one. The AI will immediately start transcribing.

---

## 🎬 Subtitle Studio

Once a file is transcribed, you'll be taken to the Editor.

### 1. Video/Audio Preview
- **Play/Pause Button:** Controls media playback.
- **Caption Overlay:** Shows how your subtitles look in real-time.
- **Waveform:** A visual representation of your audio. Click anywhere to jump to that time.

### 2. Caption Editor (Right Sidebar)
- **Edit Text:** Click on any caption block to change the text.
- **Timestamps:** View the start and end time of each block. Click a block to jump the video to that moment.
- **Add/Delete:** Use the `+` button to add new captions or the trash icon to remove them.

### 3. Style Panel
- **Font Family:** Choose between Modern (Inter), Tech (Space Grotesk), Mono, or Serif.
- **Font Size:** Adjust text size using the slider.
- **Colors:** Change text and background colors using the color pickers.
- **Position:** Move captions to the Top, Middle, or Bottom of the screen.
- **Presets:** Quickly apply "TikTok Bold" or "YouTube Minimal" styles.

---

## 🎵 Lyrics Lab

Similar to Subtitle Studio, but optimized for music.

### 1. LRC Generation
- **How it works:** The AI identifies word-level timestamps.
- **Export:** Click the **Export** button and select **LRC (Lyrics)** to download a time-synced lyrics file compatible with most music players.

---

## 🎧 DJ Tools

Tools for music analysis and video synchronization.

### 1. BPM & Key Analysis
- **Action:** Upload a track to see its estimated BPM and musical key.
- **Result:** Helps you organize your library and plan transitions.

### 2. Video Sync Speed
- **Action:** Enter a "Base BPM" (e.g., 100) and a "Target BPM" (e.g., 128).
- **Result:** The AI calculates the exact speed multiplier (e.g., 1.28x) you need to apply to a video to make it sync perfectly with your track.

---

## 📤 Exporting

When you are finished editing:
1. Click the **Export** button in the bottom control bar.
2. Choose **SRT** for standard video subtitles.
3. Choose **LRC** for music lyrics.
4. The file will download immediately to your computer.
